About
Us

Sincere Hot Spring & Resort is located on Deyang Road in Jiaoxi Township, Yilan County, within easy walking distance of Jiaoxi Old Street and Tangweigou Hot Spring Park. Every room features a private stone soaking bath fed by certified natural sodium bicarbonate spring water — known as the "Beauty Bath" — which leaves your skin soft and smooth no matter how long you soak, making it one of the most beloved spring waters in Jiaoxi.

We offer four curated room types, from cozy double rooms to a spacious quadruple suite with a private terrace. The entire property uses smart self check-in, so you enjoy complete freedom and privacy throughout your stay. Pets are warmly welcomed, and we partner with nearby breakfast shops, parking lots, and local vehicle rental services to make every Jiaoxi journey effortless.

Guest Notice & House Rules

Please read the following before check-in to ensure a smooth and pleasant stay

There is no elevator — the 2nd and 3rd floors are reached by stairs.
You may briefly stop your car at the front door to unload luggage (please don't block the entrance of the Japanese restaurant next door).
There is no front desk; we use self check-in. For any questions, please contact us via WhatsApp.
Forgotten or lost items
We are unable to mail back items left behind, and we cannot take responsibility for lost valuables. Please look after your personal belongings during your stay. Thank you.
